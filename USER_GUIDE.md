# HuddleUp user guide

HuddleUp helps a campus community share learning resources and connect with classmates. The screens and available actions may vary with the service configuration and your account permissions.

## Create an account and sign in

1. Open HuddleUp and choose an available sign-in option.
2. Complete the requested account or campus-verification steps.
3. Add your profile details and study information when prompted.

Email, Google, Facebook, phone, or other sign-in options require the operator to enable and configure the corresponding provider. If an option is unavailable, contact the person or organization that operates your HuddleUp service.

## Share and find study material

- Use the community feed to browse campus posts. Posts can be associated with a course, department, or university.
- When creating a post, choose a category such as Notes, Lecture, Question, or Study tip, then add text and an optional attachment.
- Use the feed's search and category filters to find material. You can like, comment on, follow authors, or save posts where those controls are available.
- Use Discover and profiles to find classmates and view shared profile information.

Only share material you have the right to distribute. Posts and profile information may be visible to other users in the selected campus audience.

## Messages and groups

- Open Messages to find or start a direct conversation with a classmate.
- Create a group by selecting classmates and giving the group a name. The current app limits groups to eight members, including you.
- Messages and conversation details are stored by the configured HuddleUp service. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information in a chat.

## Videos, notifications, and your profile

- Reels provides a short-video browsing screen when content is available.
- Notifications shows activity surfaced by the app.
- Profile and profile-editing screens let you view or update account details and shared profile information.
- Settings includes appearance and reading options, sign-out, and an account-deletion action.

## Report or block content

Use the available report or block controls on posts, profiles, or messages to raise a concern or limit contact. Authorized moderators may review reports and manage content or accounts. If you believe there is an urgent safety issue, contact your campus or local support channel as well.

## Account deletion

Open Settings and choose **Delete account**, then follow the confirmation steps in the app. If the option fails or you cannot sign in, contact the operator of your HuddleUp service. Data deletion timing and retention may depend on the service operator and its backups.

## Troubleshooting

- **Sign-in fails:** Check your connection and make sure the selected sign-in provider is enabled for this deployment.
- **Campus verification is unavailable:** Ask the service operator whether your campus and account type are supported.
- **Posts, messages, or attachments do not load:** The backend or media-storage service may be unavailable; try again later or contact the operator.
- **Photo or document selection is blocked:** Review Android's permission prompts and grant access only if you want to select media or files.
